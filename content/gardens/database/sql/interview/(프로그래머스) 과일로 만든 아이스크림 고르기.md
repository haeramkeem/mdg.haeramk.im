- 그냥 `JOIN` 해서 풀면 된다.

```sql
SELECT f.flavor
FROM first_half f, icecream_info i
WHERE f.flavor = i.flavor
AND f.total_order > 3000
AND i.ingredient_type = 'fruit_based'
ORDER BY total_order DESC;
```
